🧮 Quantum Sims™ — Computational Research

Purpose

Explore computational simulation, quantum-inspired methods, mathematical modeling, and comparisons with classical approaches.

Research Question

Under what conditions do different simulation methods provide measurable benefits for a defined computational problem?

Objectives

- Document mathematical models and assumptions.
- Build reproducible computational experiments.
- Compare methods against appropriate classical baselines.
- Report accuracy, computational cost, and limitations.

Methodology

Document algorithms, equations, parameters, software dependencies, datasets, and evaluation metrics.

Validation

Test implementations, compare results with trusted baselines where available, document errors and uncertainty, and provide reproducible instructions.

Limitations

Simulation results alone do not establish physical quantum behavior or quantum advantage. Performance claims require appropriate benchmarks and evidence.

Status

Initial research framework — experiments and validation pending.
